How to prepare Jollof Rice

Hi, I'm Marvellous, i will be showing you how to prepare Jollof Rice but the measurement is for 4-6servings. I will be listing the ingredients and the preparation steps.

Ingredients:

  • 4 cups of rice
  • 4 fresh tomatoes
  • 2 scotch bonnet(ata rodo)
  • 1 small tin(70g) of Tomato paste
  • 2 Onions
  • 1/2 cup of Vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon of Thyme
  • 1 teaspoon of Curry powder
  • 3 cloves of Garlic
  • 1 Ginger
  • 2 Seasoning cubes
  • Salt (To taste)
  • Water (3-4cups, as needed)

Preparation Steps:

  1. Blend your pepper
    Blend the Tomatoes, one onion, garlic, and ginger until its smooth.
  2. Fry the tomato paste
    Heat your vegetable oil in a pot. Slice the remaining onion and fry a little. Add tomato paste and fry about 3 minutes, stirring reqularly.
  3. Add the blended pepper
    Pour your blended mixture. Stir and fry for about 5-10 minutes until it is thoroughly mixed.
  4. Season the stew
    Add curry, thyme, seasoning cubes and salt. Add your water and stir.Taste to check the seasoning.
  5. Add the rice
    Wash your rice thoroughly and drain. Pour it into the stew and mix properly.
  6. Cook until done
    Cover the pot tightly and cook for approximately 25-30 minutes. Check occasionally, if the rice is still hard and the water has dried up, add a little water.

Cooking Terms

Blend:
To mix the ingredients until smooth.
Drain:
Ensure the rice doesn't contain water.
Stir:
Gently turning and not in hurry.
Fry:
Fry the tomato paste properly to remove its raw taste in oil.
Cook:
Cook on low heat after adding rice.
